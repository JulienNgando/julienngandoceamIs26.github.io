what_i_learned: |
  Looking back across this entire summer research experience, it is incredible to see how much ground we covered from day one to the final symposium. Diving deep into atmospheric $PM_{2.5}$ prediction forced me to grow far beyond basic model implementation. I had to master the subtleties of sequential architectures like LSTMs and Bi-GRUs, navigate complex feature engineering with sliding windows, and troubleshoot real-world data pipeline challenges like target leakage and array indexing bugs. Learning how to rigorously validate results rather than taking high performance metrics at face value was easily one of the most important technical lessons of the summer.

  Equally meaningful was the collaborative and communicative side of the research process. Working alongside my graduate mentor, project partners, and presenting our findings to Dr. Mack, Michael, and Elijah taught me how to turn complex machine learning concepts into clear, actionable scientific narrative. From fine-tuning LaTeX paper drafts to delivering presentations in front of our peers, this summer truly shaped how I approach scientific inquiry, technical writing, and public speaking. It was a complete end-to-end experience that bridged the gap between raw data and impactful research.
blockers: |
  Packing up our project workspace, archiving scripts, and writing comprehensive documentation so that future researchers can seamlessly pick up our codebase took longer than anticipated. It was surprisingly challenging to distill weeks of iterative experiments, code tweaks, and model iterations into a clean, easy-to-follow repository README before officially logging off.
reflection: |
  This summer was a transformative milestone for my growth as both a researcher and developer. Overcoming technical roadblocks, refining our machine learning pipeline, and presenting our findings reinforced my passion for computational problem-solving. I am stepping away from this program with stronger technical confidence, incredible mentorship memories, and a solid foundation that I am excited to carry forward into my next academic and research endeavors.
